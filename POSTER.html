HealthNexus

Unified Outpatient Appointment Booking, Real-Time Queue Triage, 108 Emergency Ambulance Dispatch, & Regional Blood Bank Inventory System.

STACK Node.js 20 • Express • SQLite3
DEPLOYMENT Google Cloud Run (Alpine)

Healthcare Delivery Crises

  • OPD Waiting Room Chaos: 2–4 hour queue congestion with zero real-time visibility into doctor availability or consultation tokens.
  • Disconnected Emergency Transit: Disconnect between 108/102 callers, paramedics, and hospital ER triage desks causes fatal delays.
  • Opaque Blood Inventory: Surgical suites waste vital minutes manually calling external blood banks for rare PRBC units and platelets.

HealthNexus 5-Pillar Architecture

  • 1. Specialist Appointment Booking: Guaranteed tokens (Q-01) & INR consultation fees across 8 clinical departments.
  • 2. Live Queue & Patient Triage: Real-time ticket tracking, priority elderly/pediatric tags, and receptionist walk-in entry.
  • 3. 108/102 Emergency Ambulance SOS: Automated ALS/BLS fleet dispatch with driver contacts, telemetry, and 5-stage transit radar.
  • 4. Regional Blood Bank Index: Real-time cryogenic vault inventory across all 8 blood groups with low-stock warnings (≤ 5 units).
  • 5. Hospital Command Center: Central admin triage manifest, stock adjustment, and emergency surgical blood requisition pipeline.
